# Hi 👋, I'm Ankit Jindal, DevOps Engineer

## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **DevOps Engineer** focused on **Cloud & Site Reliability**, currently building and running infrastructure at **Eventstrat Labs**.

I own end-to-end DevOps for **PACE**, a multi-tenant SaaS platform (website/app builder, admin & exhibitor portals, AI chatbot) spanning **30+ microservices across 3 environments**. Alongside the product, I deliver zero-downtime infrastructure for **national-scale government events** — including the India AI Impact Summit, Bharat Tex, IHE, Aahar, UPITS, and IEW.

I care about the things that keep systems healthy and teams fast: **Kubernetes, AWS, CI/CD security hardening, observability, and cloud cost optimization**.

### What I've Been Working On
- 🚀 Migrated production infrastructure from **EC2 → EKS**, including a **zero-downtime database migration**.
- 📈 Rolled out **OpenTelemetry tracing** with APM dashboards, standardized autoscaling & health checks — sustaining **98%+ availability** and cutting recurring alerts from several a day to about once a week.
- ⚡ Cut frontend build times from **8–10 min → 4–5 min** and embedded **Trivy + SonarQube** with enforced quality gates, helping resolve 200+ critical and 500+ high-severity vulnerabilities.
- 🔐 Hardened security groups & S3 policies, moved to VPN-only internal access, and migrated secrets into a managed store in support of **SOC 2** compliance.
- 💰 Reduced cloud spend — S3 from **~$130/day → $27/day**, nightly scale-down of ~70% of worker nodes, and **Kubecost**-based cost-per-tenant visibility.
- 🧯 Cut CPU utilization from **99% → 34%** under peak event load by optimizing slow DB queries with developers.
